# CodeX Energy Drink Market Analysis

## Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Codebasics Resume Project Challenge**. The objective was to analyze survey data and generate actionable business insights for **CodeX**, an imaginary German energy drink company planning to strengthen its presence in the Indian market.

Using Power BI, I transformed survey responses from 10,000 participants across 10 Indian cities into meaningful visualizations and strategic recommendations for the marketing team.

## Business Problem

CodeX recently launched its energy drink in India and conducted a large-scale consumer survey to understand:

* Brand awareness
* Consumer preferences
* Purchase behavior
* Marketing effectiveness
* Market opportunities

The goal was to support decision-making for increasing market share and improving marketing strategies.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Data Cleaning
* Data Visualization

## Key Insights

* Identified the most influential factors affecting energy drink purchases.
* Analyzed consumer demographics and consumption patterns.
* Evaluated brand awareness across different customer segments.
* Compared CodeX's market position against competitors.
* Recommended strategies to improve brand visibility and customer acquisition.

## Dashboard Features

* Demographics Analysis
* Consumer Preferences
* Brand Awareness Tracking
* Purchase Behavior Analysis
* Competitor Insights
* Marketing Recommendations

## Project Outcome

The dashboard converts raw survey data into business-focused insights, helping stakeholders understand customer behavior and identify growth opportunities in the Indian energy drink market.

## About the Challenge

This project was developed as part of the **Codebasics Resume Project Challenge**, which focuses on solving real-world business problems using data analytics and visualization techniques.
